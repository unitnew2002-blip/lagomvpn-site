Questions before you install

LagomVPN is a VPN (virtual private network) app for phones, computers, TVs and routers. It encrypts your traffic and sends it through a server location you choose, so your connection stays private and keeps working on restricted networks.

There's no catch. The free plan gives you 10 GB, 1 device and 3 locations with no ads, no trackers and no logs, and you don't need a card to start. We earn money from PRO subscriptions, not from your data.

Any VPN adds a short detour for your traffic, so some speed loss is normal. LagomVPN connects you to the nearest location automatically to keep that loss small, and PRO adds priority servers for streaming and calls.

No. We don't log the sites you visit, what you download or what you do online. We only process the minimum needed to run the service, such as how much of your free data you've used. The details are in our Privacy Policy.

LagomVPN is a VLESS VPN. VLESS is a lightweight, modern protocol whose traffic is harder to tell apart from regular encrypted web traffic, so restrictive networks find it harder to spot and block than standard VPN protocols. It's included on every plan, including Free.

The free plan covers 1 device. PRO includes unlimited devices on one account, so you can connect every phone, laptop and TV at home.
